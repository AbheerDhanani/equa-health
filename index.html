Honest Explainer

Why does it cost less?

The three-picture explainer: answering the price doubt honestly without using price as the hook.

1

Discovery was paid 20 years ago

When a new medicine is invented, the originator company receives a 20-year patent monopoly to recover laboratory research costs. Once that patent expires, the molecular recipe is legally open for anyone to make.

2

The brand pays for sales & ads

Branded pharmaceutical companies employ large teams of sales representatives, give gifts, print glossy brochures, and sponsor conferences to keep doctors writing their brand name.

3

The medicine inside stays the same

EQUA doesn’t employ sales teams or run brand promotions. You only pay for the raw chemical molecule, licensed clean-room manufacturing, and certified laboratory batch checks.

Real Doubts

Common questions

Short, honest answers—including asking your doctor first.

Is it the same as my brand?

Yes. The active therapeutic molecule, the dosage strength, and the rate of absorption into your body are identical. Trademark law requires different packaging and tablet shapes, but the chemical remedy inside is the same.

Why does my doctor write a brand name?

Doctors see many patients daily and naturally write the familiar brand names they learned in residency or saw from pharmaceutical representatives. When you politely ask your doctor if the generic salt is fine, most physicians readily agree.

Can I switch?

Ask your doctor first. For chronic maintenance medicines like blood pressure, cholesterol, and diabetes, switching to the equal salt is standard and safe, but your doctor should always confirm that the strength and release profile match your clinical plan.

Regulatory Evidence Summary for Physicians

Bioequivalence & Pharmacopoeial Quality Standards

EQUA dispenses generic formulations complying with the bioequivalence criteria defined under Schedule M of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules (India) and WHO guidelines.

• Bioequivalence Margin: 90% Confidence Interval for AUC and Cmax within 80.00% – 125.00%.
• Dissolution Standard: In-vitro profile similarity factor (f2) > 50 against originator standards.
• Facilities: Sourced strictly from WHO-GMP compliant manufacturing facilities.
